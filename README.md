# InterviewCoach 🎯
### AI-Powered Interview Simulator

Practice job interviews with real AI feedback. Not a chatbot — a structured evaluator that scores your answers like a real hiring manager.

🔗 **[Live Demo](https://interviewcoach-rho.vercel.app)** 

---

## What it does

- Generates role-specific interview questions (Frontend, Backend, Full Stack, etc.)
- Evaluates your answers across 4 dimensions: STAR Structure, Specificity, Relevance, Confidence
- Quotes your exact words back with targeted feedback
- Shows a full performance summary after 5 questions

## Why it's different

Most interview prep tools give generic tips. InterviewCoach reads *your actual answer* and tells you specifically what you said that was weak — and how to fix it.
